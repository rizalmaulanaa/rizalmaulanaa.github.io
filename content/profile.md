Hi, I'm Rizal — a Technical Solutions Architect at PLN Icon Plus (through PT. Indo Koppe Enjiniring). I design AI solution architectures for enterprise use cases involving **LLMs, OCR, and computer vision**, and work with stakeholders and engineering teams to turn business requirements into AI systems that hold up in production.

Before that I was a Senior AI Engineer at PT. Semesta Integrasi Digital, building a document comparison system and a RAG-based customer service chatbot. Earlier, as an AI Engineer at PLN Icon Plus, I built a nationwide OCR and embedding pipeline for document digitization, an AI assistant with speech-to-speech for internal document workflows, CCTV-based vehicle counting and speed estimation, and safety hazard detection — and led the on-premise deployment of large language models.

My path started in data: I worked as a data engineer at BRI and Telkom Indonesia, building ETL pipelines, dashboards, and web crawlers. I hold a Master's in Computer Science from **Universitas Indonesia** and a Bachelor's from **Universitas Brawijaya**. My graduate research focused on biomedical image analysis — automated segmentation of white matter hyperintensities in brain MRI — which led to two publications in 2021 and a talk at Universitas Indonesia. I also spent three semesters there as a teaching assistant for Advanced Machine Learning.
